Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Cheats switch off trophies and saving — keep them off your trophy save. Sources: KeenGamer, Output Lag.

Stage 1 — Play the story

Finish all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. Story trophies pop as you go. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (after Down to Earth, find her in Jason’s kitchen in the morning and follow her to the balcony) — it feeds Positive Mental Attitude, the one trophy that can be missed.

Stage 2 — Reach 100% completion

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist: robberies, fencing, collectibles, races, shooting range golds, drone trials, stunts and challenges. You only need portions of most categories — check the in-game totals before grinding everything. This stage is most of the 200 hours.

Stage 3 — Clean up the rest

Side-story finales, Strangers & Freaks strands, activities, robberies and miscellaneous trophies. Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method yet at launch — this guide says so honestly where that is the case.

Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Earn the other 54 trophies and the Platinum pops automatically. Congratulations — you’re a Leonida Legend.
